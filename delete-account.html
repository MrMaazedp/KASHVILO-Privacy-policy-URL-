Account & Data Deletion – Kashvilo

App Name: Kashvilo (com.aistudio.moneymate.pkrtvd)
Developer Contact: mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com

How to Delete Your Account & Data

Option 1: Request via Email

  1. Send an email to mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com from the Google email address linked to your Kashvilo account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request – Kashvilo.
  3. We will permanently delete your account and associated cloud data within 7 days and send you a confirmation email.

Option 2: Clear Financial Data Inside the App

  1. Open Kashvilo and go to Account & Settings (Profile tab).
  2. Tap Clear All Data to immediately remove your stored transactions, budgets, and savings goals without deleting your sign-in account.

Data That Is Deleted

  • Google Sign-In profile information (display name, email address, Firebase User ID)
  • All synced transactions, budgets, savings goals, and currency preferences stored in Cloud Firestore

Data Retention

Once an account deletion request is processed, all personal and financial data listed above is permanently deleted with 0 days additional retention. Local guest/offline data stored only on your device can be removed anytime by tapping Clear All Data or uninstalling the app.
