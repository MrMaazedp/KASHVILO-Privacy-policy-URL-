Thank you for choosing Kashvilo ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information (Optional Google Sign-In): If you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, and Firebase User ID strictly to authenticate your account and sync your records across devices. You can also use Kashvilo in Guest/Demo mode without signing in.
  • Financial Records You Enter: Income transactions, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and currency preferences that you manually create are stored securely in your private account (Google Cloud Firestore or local device storage).
  • Camera & Receipt Images: When you use the Receipt Scanner, the App requests access to your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) or uses the Android Photo Picker so you can capture or select a receipt image. Text extraction is processed on-device via Google ML Kit OCR (with optional AI parsing). Receipt photos are not sold or used for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide core app functionality:

  • Track and display your income, expenses, budgets, and savings accounts.
  • Scan receipts to automatically fill store names, totals, dates, and categories.
  • Sync and back up your personal finance records when signed into your account.
  • Provide AI-powered financial summaries when you explicitly ask the AI Assistant.

3. Third-Party Services

Kashvilo uses trusted Google services to operate securely:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: For user login and private cloud data storage.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition: For on-device receipt scanning.
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini): For user-requested AI financial advice and insights.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your records remain in your account only as long as you choose to keep them. You can delete individual transactions or wipe all financial data anytime inside the App via Account & Settings → Clear All Data & Reset to 0.

To request complete deletion of your account and cloud records, please email us at mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com with the email address associated with your account.

5. Children's Privacy

Kashvilo is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
