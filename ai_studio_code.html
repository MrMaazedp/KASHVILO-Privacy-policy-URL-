Privacy Policy – Kashvilo

Effective Date: November 18, 2026
App Name: Kashvilo (com.aistudio.moneymate.pkrtvd)
Developer Contact: mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Kashvilo collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Offline / Guest Mode: You can use Kashvilo without signing in. Financial entries created in Guest Mode are stored locally on your device.
  • Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your display name, email address, and Firebase User ID to authenticate your account and sync your records.
  • Financial & Budget Records: Transactions, categories, budgets, and savings goals you enter are stored locally or synced to Google Cloud Firestore when signed in.
  • Camera & Receipt Photos: When you use the Receipt Scanner, camera or selected gallery images are processed using on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit) and optional AI assistance to extract store names and totals. Photos are not permanently stored on our servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kashvilo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (App Open, Banner, and Interstitial ads). Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, cookies, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

Kashvilo relies on trusted Google services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase AI, Google ML Kit, and Google AdMob. Data transmitted to these services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can clear all stored financial records or permanently delete your account directly inside the app under Account & Settings, or request deletion online by visiting our Account & Data Deletion page or emailing mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Kashvilo is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mscreativelabs2006@gmail.com.
